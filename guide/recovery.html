I ran a command from a website. What now?

Maybe a page said "verify you are human" and told you to press Win+R, paste and press Enter. Do these steps in order. The first hour matters most. You can tick the boxes as you go.

Why this order? These attacks install an "infostealer". It copies the sign-ins saved in your browser and apps. With those, criminals can open your accounts without your password and without the code from your phone. So first you cut off the stolen sign-ins from a clean device, then you clean the computer.

Disconnect the affected computer

Right now, 2 minutes
2

Secure your email first, from your phone

Next 15 minutes, on a phone or another clean device

Your email can reset all your other passwords, so it goes first.

3

Sign out everywhere else

Next 20 minutes, still from your phone
Cannot find an option? Menu names change often. Search the app's settings for "devices", "sessions", "logged in" or "security".
Why signing out matters: a password change alone does not always end a session that is already open. Signing out of all sessions makes the stolen sign-ins useless. Do both.

Four habits that stop this attack
  • Real "I am not a robot" checks never ask you to press Win+R, open Terminal, or paste anything. If a page asks, close it.
  • Read any command before you run it. If you do not understand it, do not run it.
  • When you are not sure, act as if it was an attack. Signing out of all sessions takes five minutes.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication on every account that offers it.
